Lesson 23 · Word formation

What you will learn

IELTS rewards a wide range of vocabulary. One word gives you a whole family: employ, employer, employee, employment, unemployment, unemployed. This lesson shows you the prefixes and suffixes that build the family.

You will learnExample
prefixes with meaninganti-, co-, ex-, inter-, mis-, over-, post-, pre-, re-, semi-, under-
opposite prefixesdis-, il-, im-, in-, ir-, non-, un-
peopleteacher, motorist, contestant, librarian, trainee
nounsrefusal, acceptance, enjoyment, loneliness, loyalty
adjectivesfamous, heroic, expensive, careful, healthy, likeable
verbswiden, legalise, simplify
🧩
In plain wordsLook at the ending: it tells you the part of speech (noun, adjective, verb). Look at the beginning: it changes the meaning.
IELTS tipIn Writing Task 2 do not repeat the same word. Use the family: Pollution is harmful. Polluted air harms health. Governments must reduce pollutants. In Reading, a gap often needs a noun, adjective or adverb, so check the part of speech.

Ask yourselfWhat is the gap? Noun (after the / a / an / possessive)? Adjective (before a noun, or after be)? Adverb (describes a verb)? Verb (after to / will)? Choose the ending after you decide.
