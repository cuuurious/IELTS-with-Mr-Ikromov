Lesson 29 · Prepositions

What you will learn

Prepositions are small words with a big effect. Some follow rules (time and place). Many others must simply be learnt with their word: good at, afraid of, depend on, die of. This lesson gives you both.

You will learnExample
time: at / in / onat 6 · on Monday · in July
place: at / in / onat the airport · in Paris · on the left
movementacross the river · through the tunnel
transportby bus · on the bus · in a taxi
dependent prepositionsgood at · fond of · depend on · die of
fixed phrasesby accident · on purpose · out of order
phrasal verbsturn down · fall out · take after
📍
In plain wordsRules for time and place. Memory for the rest. Don't translate preposition by preposition: Uzbek and Russian do not match English. Learn word + preposition as one piece.
IELTS tipWrong prepositions are among the most frequent small errors in Writing and Speaking. Check them in your essay: depend on, interested in, different from, result in, the increase in, an advantage of, in the chart, by 2030.

Part 1

at, in, on (time)

A point, a period, a day.

at · in · on

atinon
at 10.30 · at noon / night / midnightin the morning / afternoon / eveningon Monday · on Friday night
at Christmas / Easterin January · in (the) winteron Christmas Day · on July 30th
at lunch / dinner (time)in 1992 · in the 19th centuryon a summer afternoon
at the weekend (BrE) · at that timein two hours (= two hours from now)on that day · on my birthday
at the moment · at presenton Easter Sunday

More time words

  • from … to / till / until shows duration: She works from 9 to 5.
  • in = how long it takes, or after a time: He finished the test in half an hour. The flat will be ready in a month.
  • Never at, in, on before today, tomorrow, yesterday, tonight, this, next, last, every, one, any, each, some, all: She is leaving next Sunday.

Typical mistakes

I will see you at next Monday.
I will see you next Monday.
My birthday is in 5th May.
My birthday is on 5th May.
At the morning I run.
In the morning I run.
Part 2

at, in, on (place) · movement · transport

Where? And in which direction?

at · in · on

UseExamples
ata point or position; where an event takes place; function of a building; house number; someone's house or shopat the door · at the party · at school / work / home · at the airport · at 18 King Street · at Joan's · at the doctor's
ininside; towns, countries, continents; bed, hospital, prison; a street (BrE); book, newspaper, picture, sky, worldin Milan · in Egypt · in the middle · in hospital · in King Street
onon a surface; a line; floors; islands and farms; left / righton the table · on the 4th floor · on an island · on a farm · on my left · on the platform · on the beach

In = the building itself (It was hot in the cinema). At = its normal function (They are at the cinema = watching a film).

Other place and movement words

  • above / over · below / under · next to / by / beside · between (two) · among (many) · opposite · in front of · behind · inside · outside
  • along · across · through · over · under · past · round / around · up · down · towards · from … to · into · out of · onto · off
  • climb over a wall · walk past the shops · swim across a river · run down the stairs · walk towards me · get out of bed · put it onto the table

Transport

  • by + transport, no article: by bus, by train, by car, by air
  • With a / the / my / possessive: on the bus / train / plane / ship (you can walk inside) and in a car / taxi / helicopter: on the eight o'clock bus · in a taxi · in Richard's car

Typical mistakes

We arrived to the airport.
We arrived at the airport. (in a town: arrive in London)
I go to work with the bus.
I go to work by bus. / on the bus.
She lives in 18 King Street.
She lives at 18 King Street.
